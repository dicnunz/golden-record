# Source material

The Golden Record is a curated collection containing work by many authors. Public availability and NASA hosting do not establish that every recorded photograph is public domain. Original creators retain applicable rights. The recovered planes are included as evidence of this decoding experiment; check the rights of individual source works before other reuse. NASA's Golden Record pages provide image credits.

- NASA engraved cover and explanation: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/golden-record-cover/
- NASA image credits and analog encoding: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/golden-record-contents/images/
- Public stereo waveform preservation copy: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/0B0Swx_1rwA6XcFFLc29ncFJSZmM

The full waveform is not redistributed. `scripts/fetch.py` retrieves the pinned public copy. Dependencies and DejaVu fonts retain their own licenses. No image-generation service is used.
